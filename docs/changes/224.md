Latte, GitHub Light, Gruvbox Light or Solarized Light; Atlas Dark, Catppuccin Mocha, Dracula, Gruvbox Dark, Nord or

View › Light or Dark, or ◐ in the status bar switches between the two. Atlas Light and Atlas Dark look as they did.
