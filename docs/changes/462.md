A route draws what its steps make of each other's results. An error that only leaves, handed on from step to step up to
the route's border, is no part of that: it is counted on each step that hands it on (`parse +1 error`) and on the frame of an
opened step, and the boxes and their lines are gone. An error a later step takes, a handler that uses what it caught,
stays a box. A click on a count, or the chip `1 counted on the step ✕` above the picture, draws the errors as boxes
again; `errors: true` in a view does the same.
