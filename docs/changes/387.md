Where two types go by one name, the view put the last part of each one's module before it. Two modules can share that
part too: `orders.model.Frame` and `billing.model.Frame` were both drawn `model.Frame`, and so were their steps
(`model.Frame()`, `model.Frame.total`). Each type now carries the fewest of its last modules that no other type of its
name stands in last: `orders.model.Frame`, `billing.model.Frame`, and still `dense.Rows` beside `sparse.Rows` where the
last part tells them apart. A sum written in place, which stands in no module, is told apart by its variants where its
written name is shared. The page, `route`, `check` and `changes` say it alike. On Atlas itself, `python.body.Body`,
`rust.body.Body` and `typescript.body.Body` were all `body.Body`.

On side A of a page of changes, a node B renamed is drawn by the name A's reading gave it, where it was a cut of its
old id. A sum written in place (`orders.Frame | orders.Missing`) is now found renamed with the variant that was renamed
(`Missing` to `Absent`); its old id cut at the last dot read `Missing)`.
