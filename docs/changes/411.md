Opening a package in the overview merged its functions that touch nothing of their own into one box, as if they drew the
same lines, and the calls to and from them were left out: in `shop`, `total, report` and `price, tax` stood as two boxes with
no line between them or from `$ shop`. The calls are lines of the overview's plan, so two functions are one box only where
they call and are called alike, and a call to one of them is a call to the box it joined. Now `$ shop` calls `total` and
`report`, which call `price` and `tax`. On Atlas itself, 84 of the 584 views `walk` opens lacked lines this way; none does now.
