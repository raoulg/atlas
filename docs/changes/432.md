The sidebar of Studio (Atlas view, History, files, lessons) takes 264 pixels from the picture. Now the button at its
top edge (or ⌘B, Ctrl+B on Windows and Linux, View › Toggle Sidebar) folds it to a narrow rail that keeps
the two section icons, ◇ and ◷, which still switch the view. Unfolding gives the sidebar back as it was: the file tree
keeps its open folders and its place. Studio remembers the choice for the next start, and the button says *Show* or
*Hide* with the shortcut in its title and tells assistive technology whether the sidebar is expanded.

Kind: UI.
