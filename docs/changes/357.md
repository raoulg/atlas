Where an arm of a choice ran a step and then a choice of its own (`if KEEP: open_shelf(); if REPORT: keep_report()`),
the page drew one frame, `one of ● KEEP ● KEEP · REPORT`, as if the two calls were alternatives, although both run where
`REPORT` holds. The inner choice is now a frame inside the arm, `only when REPORT` within `only when KEEP`, after the step
before it, in the arm's colour. Every frame of a choice now holds alternatives only. An arm that runs nothing but one
choice is that choice: an `else` whose only statement is an `if`, or a chain of guards with nothing between them, stays
one frame with every alternative in it, as an `elif` does. The same holds in Python, Rust and TypeScript.
