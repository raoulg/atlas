area: cli               # also studio

pictures: []

Each build of Atlas keeps its own index, so the first `atlas serve` after an update reads everything, while the starts
before it were quick. The bars took the middle of all recent starts, ran full at once and waited at 99%. Now a start
that finds the index of its build and one that does not are timed apart (`.atlas/timings.json`: `warm` and `cold`), and
each bar keeps pace with starts of its own kind. Nothing in the picture changes; on Atlas itself the first reading after
a build is expected to take what the last cold one took (about 80 s for its code) instead of a few milliseconds.
