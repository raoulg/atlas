The text of a route and the words of a finding name a function and a type as the picture draws it (`model::said`). An
initializer is the call to its class: a finding says `billing.model.Frame()` where it said `Frame.__init__`, and a step
the text sends on (`→ see`), or lists in `choices`, says `Frame()` where it said `Frame`. Where a step's signature
follows, the text writes the call with what it takes, `Frame(rows)`, as before. A class whose name another type shares
carries its module (#387), in a step, in what a step gives or changes, and in a finding. A finding counts those names
over the repository's parts together, as the page draws them (the page, MCP `check`), and `changes` says both
readings' findings in the names of their union, so that a finding is not raised and resolved because another type
came to share a name with one it names.

`engine/tests/one_name.rs` (two `Frame`s, in `shop.orders.model` and `shop.billing.model`):
