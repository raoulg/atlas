The version at the right of Studio's status bar, `Atlas Studio x.y.z`, is a button: a click shows What's New for the
build that runs, the same page as the menu's What's New in Atlas Studio x.y.z and the button in Settings. That is the
release's page of notes with its pictures, or its release on GitHub, or for a build that is no release yet the commits
it is made of.
