A button beside the zoom buttons, `round lines`, draws every line of the picture with arcs where it had corners, and
`square lines` draws the corners again. The route of a line is the layout's in both shapes: the boxes stand where they
stood and each line leaves and arrives where it did, so the two pictures differ in the shape of the lines alone. The
choice is kept in the browser and is no part of a view: a link to a view opens in the shape its reader chose.
