A switch of two small pictures beside the zoom buttons, a line with corners and a line with an arc, draws every line of
the picture in the shape picked; the one in use is the dark one. The route of a line is the layout's in both shapes: the boxes stand where they
stood and each line leaves and arrives where it did, so the two pictures differ in the shape of the lines alone. The
choice is kept in the browser and is no part of a view: a link to a view opens in the shape its reader chose.
