The overview of atlas-engine with its `tests` package open took 10.7 s to draw (5.5 s before #277 added three types to
it) and now takes 1.0 s. Within a layer the viewer has ELK keep the order the code gives the boxes and lines (model
order); ELK sorts each layer against every line through it, which grows with the square of the layer. In 72 of 74 views
of Atlas and vectormesh that costs nothing (with and without, the same time up to 214 lines); in the two variants of
this view, 740 and 751 lines, it is 91 to 94 % of the layout. A picture of more than 400 lines (`ORDERED_LINES` in
`viewer/index.html`) is now laid out by its crossings alone.

```text
pictures.py draw, before and after: Atlas 41 pictures, vectormesh 41: the same below the header
  (one pixel off by at most 2 of 255 in three of Atlas's, where the header names another build)
walk: Atlas 506 views, vectormesh 115, none that throws or draws nothing
topology: Atlas 142 boxes in 40 routes, vectormesh 318 in 75, nothing lost, nothing more
the tests view in the browser: 10.6 s, 10.9 s before; 1.1 s, 0.9 s after
```
