A step that runs on an instance it was not handed otherwise says the instance by name, `encoder.save`, and draws no line
from it. Opened, its body reached the instance through its fields (`self.codes`, `self.index.items()`), and those lines
were drawn again, into the frame, with the frame titled `Encoder.save`: opening the step drew a line the closed step did
not. Now every line a method's body draws from the instance it runs on is its running on it, closed and open alike: the
opened frame says `encoder.save` and draws no line from the encoder, as the closed step did. The other way round, a
function handed the instance (`reset(board)`) keeps its line closed, and opened the call inside that runs on it
(`board.cells.clear()`) keeps it too, where it took the line away before.

`tools/pictures.py topology` also takes a box inside a frame of a choice to the step that holds it when the box's id
names no moment (a place a step reads back of what it wrote, `v:back:..~in~STEP`), so it no longer reports a line more
for such a value.
