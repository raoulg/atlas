Studio's terminal was always under the picture. Three small buttons at the right of its bar now dock it at the bottom, on
the left or on the right, a tall panel beside the view; every window follows, and Studio keeps the choice in its settings,
so it opens where it was left after a restart. The edge that faces the view drags to resize in each position, and the
buttons take the colours of the theme in force, as the terminal does.
