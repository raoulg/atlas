The Type lattice drew only the program's classes, so the rank families of jaxtyping shapes (#298) did not show there.
Now a rank family stands above its shapes as a base stands above its subclasses (`Float Tensor · 2D` over
`batch dim`, `batch _`, `batch ndim`). Space over it shows the family, its shapes and the button that lights it, and a
double click lights its values in the Flowchart. Picking the family lights its closed boxes whole; picking one shape
opens the family and lights that shape alone. An external box that is lit is drawn solid: only a box lit in part is
dashed.
