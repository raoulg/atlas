Siblings that draw the same lines are drawn as one box: in vectormesh's `gating`, `MoE`, `Skip` and `SparseMoE` all take
a `Module` and a `Float Tensor · ≥1D` and give one back, so the package shows
`vectormesh.components.MoE, vectormesh.components.Skip +1`. Before a route is chosen, a click on such a box selected it
and did nothing else, although its `▸` promised it would open. Now it opens into its members, each drawing the lines
the box drew; a click on one of them opens it as a route, as any single box does. The members stand under *opened*,
and closing them draws the one box again.
