In a large program a step beyond the cut is drawn as the image of its body. A door that took its own tensor and a
module's tensor (`forward(x)` comparing `x` with `PAD = Tensor()`) was drawn closed as `Tensor · PAD → Block.forward`,
named as if two tensors came from beyond, and opened as `Tensor`, since the door's tensor is its caller's. Now the step
planned by itself leaves to its caller which of the values it takes by a caller's name come from beyond, and the closed
step draws `Tensor` as its opened picture does: opening it keeps every line it drew.

```text
example: engine/tests/plans/door-tensor-pad, route blocks.Block, plan cut at budget 1
  0.70.0 (eeca2256)   Tensor · PAD → Block.forward; opened: lost Tensor · PAD, more Tensor
  with #319           Tensor → Block.forward; opened: nothing lost, nothing more
```
