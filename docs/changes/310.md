The preview of a file in Studio (Markdown, HTML, source, a PDF, an image) had one width, at the right of the view. Now
it stands where the terminal can: its inner edge drags, the buttons in its head dock it on the left or the right (kept
for every window, as the terminal's dock is), fold it to a narrow bar at its side that names the open file, or let it
fill the stage while the Atlas view keeps its size underneath. A click on the bar, or a file opened from the tree,
unfolds it with its tabs as they were.
