The mouse wheel zooms the picture in every case below; before, each left it dead or overridden until a click (`+`)
put it right. A drag whose release never reaches the page (let go over Studio's bar) ends at the first move with no
button held, instead of panning with every move after it. The empty part of the state bar, the zoom's buttons, the
hint, the legend and the status bar no longer swallow the wheel. Opening a box far larger than the window leaves the
scale at the zoom's floor, where a wheel out once jumped in. A wheel made while `atlas-code serve` hands the page a new
reading is kept once the picture comes back, and the picture's svg stays the same element across that redraw. A wheel
during a camera move (`+`, fit, 1:1, the move after a render) already won in both engines; it now also interrupts the
move first.

No picture: the gestures change, not what is drawn. What shows it is a probe: the page framed cross-origin below a bar
as Studio frames it, each scenario followed by a wheel, in Chromium and WebKit (Playwright), on the base `f33f41c3` and
on this change. `k` is the scale.

| scenario | before, Chromium and WebKit | after, Chromium and WebKit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| press in the frame, release never reaches it, moves, wheel | moves pan (+110, +50) | no pan; the wheel zooms |
| wheel on the state bar's empty part, `−`, the legend, the hint, the status bar | k 1.400 → 1.400 on each | each zooms (1.400 ↔ 2.122) |
| open `atlas-engine` in a 700×420 window, then wheel out | k 0.0396, the wheel out jumps in to 0.0800 | k 0.0800, the wheel out stays |
| `atlas-code serve`: a file changes, wheel as the new reading mounts | k 1.400 → 1.400, the wheel is lost | k 1.400 → 2.122 |
| wheel 40 ms into `+` | ends at 0.845 (move alone: 1.750) | ends at 0.843 |

Not reproduced: WebKit keeping a wheel gesture on an element a redraw removed. Playwright's wheel carries no gesture
phase, so WebKit does not latch it, and a wheel after the box under the pointer was removed reached the page in both
engines. Whether Studio's trackpad gestures latch so is open.
