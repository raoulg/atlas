A place whose path its callers make whole together runs in a route only where all of them are on the calls. Atlas wrote
"both callers" in the order it read them, so `a&b` and `b&a` were two answers to one question. They are one now: the
callers of each conjunction and the alternatives are sets, written in order in the graph's JSON. The pictures stay as
they were; what the JSON writes for 13 of 292 programs (the pairs, and Atlas itself) is the same sets in order, where
an alternative written twice in two orders is written once:

```text
$ compare base 276      # atlas analyze --no-cache, the pairs and Atlas itself at ae5f9ed9
identical: 279; differ only in the order `by` is written: 13; differ otherwise: 0

diamond/typescript/shortcut, the touch of `store.Store.get`:
  before  "by": ["news.read.headlines", "news.sources.Cached.constructor&news.read.headlines",
                 "news.read.headlines&news.sources.Cached.constructor", "news.sources.Cached.constructor"]
  after   "by": ["news.read.headlines", "news.read.headlines&news.sources.Cached.constructor",
                 "news.sources.Cached.constructor"]
```

No `prepares` fold moved: no place a call only prepares listed its callers in another order than the place it
prepares.
