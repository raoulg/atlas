`atlas serve` and `atlas history` answer on this machine only, and a page of another site could still make them act: an
image pointing at `/api/look` took the look an agent left for you, and one pointing at `/export` or `/history`'s `/view`
ran a whole analysis. Now what changes something or runs an analysis is done only for Atlas's own page or for you at
the address bar: the page says so with its own header, and a link it opens by the browser's word for it
(`Sec-Fetch-Site`), which no other page can say. A request that does not come in time, or is longer than Atlas reads, is
answered with why and closed, and a server answers a bounded number of connections at a time. Nothing in the picture
changes. The answers show it:

```text
request                                               before                         after
GET /api/look, from a page of another site            200, the look taken            403
GET /export, from a page of another site              200, after a whole analysis    403
GET /export, the page's own link or the address bar   200                            200
a client that connects and sends nothing              a thread held for ever         408 after 10 s
Content-Length of 5 MiB                               the body cut to 4 MiB          413
200 headers, or a header line of 2 MiB                read whole                     431
a request line of 2 MiB                               read whole                     414
one more connection while 32 are being answered       one more thread                503
```
