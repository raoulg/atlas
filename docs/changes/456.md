A step that returns what one of its calls gives now draws that value leaving through its frame, also where the
step's written return is another shape of the same tensor than the call's. Before, a branch written as
`-> Float[Tensor, "batch 16"]` that returns `self.proj(x)`, whose `forward` gives `Float[Tensor, "*lead {self.out}"]`,
showed, opened, the call's result ending at no box, and the branch's output seemed to come from nowhere. Now the line
runs from the call's result to the branch's point on its frame, as it already did where both shapes were the same.
The closed step draws as it did, and so does every other value the step makes.
