A click on the version at the right of Studio's status bar looks for a newer build of its channel and shows it; without
one it shows what changed in the build that runs, and the status bar says it is up to date. Settings → Check for
updates writes its answer under the button, with the time it was asked, for as long as the page is open. An answer to
something asked (a look for updates, an error) stands in the status bar for 15 seconds; what Studio says meanwhile
shows once it has been read.
