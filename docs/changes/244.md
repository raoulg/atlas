Atlas drawn by itself names its places by what they hold. What `atlas view`, `serve` and an agent share is the
workbench, `.atlas/` at the top of the subject's worktree: `{subject}/.atlas/graph.json`, `vision.json`, `look.json`,
where it showed `{out}/.atlas/graph.json` and `File({a}/.git)`. A clone, a revision and a vision are each laid out under
`$TMPDIR` as their own kind (`atlas-clone-…`, `atlas-revision-…`, `atlas-vision-…`) and removed when read. The command
line keeps its last answer about updates in the same cache folder as the rest of what it keeps (on macOS
`~/Library/Caches/atlas/update.json`, where it was `~/.cache/atlas/update.json`), and Atlas Studio keeps its own in its
cache folder rather than beside the user's settings; each asks again once.
