Atlas 0.72.1 draws every tensor as its rank, `Float Tensor · 3D`, also zoomed into a route and when it is the only
shape of its rank. Everything else Atlas reads, draws and checks is the same as in 0.72.0.

## A tensor reads as its rank, wherever it is drawn

Kind: wrong

A shaped array stood in its rank family only where two shapes of that rank met: zoomed into a route the family was gone,
and a program with one 5D shape never showed `5D`. Now every shape stands closed in its rank family, in the overview and
in a route alike. Opened, the family is a frame around the shape itself, where the names of its axes show. The rank is
read off each shape alone, so a family of one is a family.

Compare: releases/0.72.1/img/425-before.png = 0.72.0: in the route of `Attention`, the shape with its axis names, `Float[Tensor, batch seq {self.hidden_size}]`; releases/0.72.1/img/425-after.png = 0.72.1: the same route, the rank `Float Tensor · 3D`
